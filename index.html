About the home

Ground Floor - Home#1

Ground Floor Home#1 is a self-contained 1500 sqft home on the ground floor — a private bedroom with an attached bathroom, a spacious living and dining hall, a fully equipped kitchen, and a terrace. It's ideal for families and groups of up to 7 travelling together, with 3 extra beds available on request for groups up to 10, close to the town's markets and 1.4 km from Virudunagar Junction Railway Station.
